Sync Page Button Test

Important: The buttons in the sync test page are hidden by default!

You need to click the "+ Add Deck" button first to reveal the form with the Create/Update buttons.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Go to: http://localhost:8080/sync-test
  2. Look for the blue button at the top right that says:
  3. Click "+ Add Deck" - This will reveal the form
  4. You'll now see:
    • Three input fields (Name, Format, Owner)
    • Two buttons at the bottom right:
  5. Fill in the form and click the green "Create" button
  6. To test Update button: Click the yellow "Edit" button on any deck in the table, and the green button will change to say "Update"

Verify Buttons Exist:

Run this test in your terminal:

curl -s http://localhost:8080/_app/immutable/nodes/6.4f6a837b.js | grep -o "Create\|Update\|Cancel" | uniq

Should output: Create, Update, Cancel
